🍵 MATCHA MAYA Interviews Cape Town Creative in Cape Town

MATCHA MAYA is on a global mission to find the best coworking spaces, ergonomic furniture, and matcha experiences. In this video call interview, she connects with Cape Town Creative (@capetowncreative), a local influencer based in Cape Town, to talk about working remotely in one of the world's most stunning cities.

📹 The Video Call

"Cape Town Creative, thanks for joining me! You're in Cape Town right now, working from Workshop17. How's the vibe there today?"

Cape Town Creative: "It's amazing and creative! Workshop17 is one of the top coworking spaces in Cape Town. Today, it's full of creatives, entrepreneurs, and digital nomads."

"Let's start with the chair you're sitting on. What's the ergonomic situation like?"

Cape Town Creative: "The chairs are comfortable ergonomic chair. I've been working here for hours and my back feels completely fine."

"Now, tell me about the desk setup—is it spacious enough for a dual-monitor workflow?"

Cape Town Creative: "Absolutely! The desks are spacious well-designed desks with power outlets. There's plenty of room for a dual-monitor setup and even some personal items."

"One of the most important things for remote work is a good internet connection. What's the average internet speed in Cape Town? Is the Wi-Fi here fast and reliable?"

Cape Town Creative: "Cape Town has 30-50 Mbps (80 Mbps at coworking). It's more than enough for video calls, streaming, and large file uploads."
