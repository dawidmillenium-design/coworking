"Amsterdam Adventures, thanks for joining me! You're in Amsterdam right now, working from B. Amsterdam. How's the vibe there today?"

Amsterdam Adventures: "Absolutely! The desks are spacious well-designed desks with power outlets. There's plenty of room for a dual-monitor setup and even some personal items."
