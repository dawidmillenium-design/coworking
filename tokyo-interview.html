🍵 MATCHA MAYA Interviews Tokyo Nomad in Tokyo

Published: July 2026 · 8 min read · Video Call Interview

MATCHA MAYA is on a global mission to find the best coworking spaces, ergonomic furniture, and matcha experiences. In this video call interview, she connects with Tokyo Nomad (@tokyonomad), a local influencer based in Tokyo, to talk about working remotely in one of the world's most efficient cities.

📹 The Video Call

"Tokyo Nomad, thanks for joining me! You're in Tokyo right now, working from Business-Airport. How's the vibe there today?"

Tokyo Nomad: "It's calm and focused! Business-Airport is one of the top coworking spaces in Tokyo. Today, it's full of creatives, entrepreneurs, and digital nomads."

"Let's start with the chair you're sitting on. What's the ergonomic situation like?"

Tokyo Nomad: "The chairs are Japanese ergonomic chair with memory foam. I've been working here for hours and my back feels completely fine."

"Now, tell me about the desk setup—is it spacious enough for a dual-monitor workflow?"

Tokyo Nomad: "Absolutely! The desks are large desks with built-in USB ports. There's plenty of room for a dual-monitor setup and even some personal items."

"One of the most important things for remote work is a good internet connection. What's the average internet speed in Tokyo? Is the Wi-Fi here fast and reliable?"

Tokyo Nomad: "Tokyo has 150-200 Mbps (1 Gbps at coworking). It's more than enough for video calls, streaming, and large file uploads."
